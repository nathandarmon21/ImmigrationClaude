# Immigration Advisory System - User Guide

Welcome to the Immigration Advisory System! This guide will help you make the most of the platform.

## Overview

The Immigration Advisory System is an AI-powered tool that helps you:
- Understand your US immigration options
- Compare different visa pathways
- Get personalized recommendations
- Receive expert-level guidance from AI

## Getting Started

### 1. Take the Assessment

The assessment helps us understand your situation and provide personalized recommendations.

**To start:**
1. Click "Start Assessment" on the home page
2. Answer questions about:
   - Your purpose for coming to/staying in the US
   - Education level
   - Field of expertise
   - Professional achievements
   - Employment situation
   - Years of experience

**Tips:**
- Be honest and accurate
- Select all achievements that apply
- If unsure, you can skip and come back later

### 2. Review Your Recommendations

After completing the assessment, you'll see personalized pathway recommendations.

**What you'll see:**
- **Fit Score**: How well each pathway matches your profile (0-100%)
- **Feasibility**: High, Medium, or Low based on your qualifications
- **Timeline**: Estimated time from start to completion
- **Cost**: Estimated total costs
- **Pros & Cons**: Specific advantages and disadvantages
- **Requirements**: What you've met vs. what's missing

**Understanding Fit Scores:**
- **70-100%**: Excellent match - you meet most requirements
- **40-69%**: Good match - possible with some work
- **0-39%**: Lower match - may have challenges

### 3. Compare Pathways

Select multiple pathways (checkbox) to compare them side-by-side.

**Comparison includes:**
- Eligibility requirements
- Timelines and costs
- Employer sponsorship needs
- Self-petition options
- Path to green card
- AI-generated expert analysis

**How to compare:**
1. Check boxes next to pathways you want to compare
2. Click "Compare Selected"
3. Review detailed comparison table
4. Read AI expert analysis

### 4. View Detailed Information

Click on any pathway to see comprehensive details:

**What's included:**
- Full description
- Detailed requirements
- Complete list of pros and cons
- Success factors
- Common denial reasons
- Personalized action plan
- Document checklist
- Official resources and links

Use the AI advisor to get answers to specific questions.

**How to use:**
1. Navigate to "Ask Questions" page
2. Type your question in the chat

4. Ask follow-up questions

**Example questions:**

## Understanding Immigration Pathways

### Work Visas (Temporary)

**H-1B - Specialty Occupation**
- Most common work visa
- Requires bachelor's degree
- Annual lottery (85,000 cap)
- 3 years, extendable to 6
- Employer sponsorship required

**O-1 - Extraordinary Ability**
- For top talent in field
- No annual cap
- High bar to qualify
- 3 years, unlimited extensions
- Can self-petition or employer sponsored

**L-1 - Intracompany Transfer**
- For employees of multinational companies
- Must work abroad for 1 year first
- No cap
- 5-7 years depending on role

### Green Cards (Permanent)

**EB-1A - Extraordinary Ability**
- Self-petition possible
- No job offer required
- Very high bar
- Fastest processing
- Meet 3 of 10 criteria

**EB-2 NIW - National Interest Waiver**
- Self-petition possible
- No job offer required
- Requires advanced degree
- Must prove national interest
- Popular for researchers, entrepreneurs

**EB-2 PERM - Traditional Employment**
- Requires employer sponsorship
- Labor certification process
- Longer timeline
- Most common employment green card

## Tips for Success

### During Assessment

1. **Be thorough** - Don't rush through questions
2. **Be honest** - Accurate info = better recommendations
3. **Document achievements** - Have evidence ready
4. **Consider all options** - Don't limit yourself

### Choosing a Pathway

1. **Match requirements** - Can you meet them?
2. **Consider timeline** - How urgent is your need?
3. **Evaluate costs** - Can you/employer afford it?
4. **Think long-term** - Does it lead where you want?
5. **Assess risks** - What are denial chances?

### Preparing to Apply

1. **Gather documents early** - Takes longer than expected
2. **Get translations** - All non-English docs need certified translations
3. **Consult attorney** - For complex cases or high stakes
4. **Start early** - Many processes have deadlines
5. **Stay organized** - Keep copies of everything

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: International Student Looking for Work

**Profile:**
- F-1 visa, finishing Master's degree
- STEM field
- Job offer from tech company

**Recommended Path:**
1. Apply for OPT/STEM OPT extension (up to 3 years)
2. Employer registers for H-1B lottery
3. If selected, transition to H-1B
4. Later pursue green card (EB-2/EB-3)

### Scenario 2: Experienced Professional with Awards

**Profile:**
- 10+ years experience
- Multiple industry awards
- Published research
- High salary

**Recommended Path:**
1. Evaluate O-1 visa (immediate)
2. Consider EB-1A green card (self-petition)
3. Alternative: EB-2 NIW if in national interest field

### Scenario 3: Startup Founder

**Profile:**
- Tech entrepreneur
- Innovative product
- Some funding
- No US job offer

**Recommended Path:**
1. Assess O-1 eligibility (if have achievements)
2. Consider EB-2 NIW (if work has national importance)
3. Alternative: Find investor for E-2 (if treaty country)
4. Long-shot: EB-1A if extraordinary achievements

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How accurate are the recommendations?

The AI analyzer uses comprehensive rules based on official requirements, but individual cases vary. Always verify with an immigration attorney.

### Can I apply for multiple visas?

Generally yes, but timing and strategy matter. Consult an attorney for multi-pathway strategies.

### How often is information updated?

Immigration policies change frequently. We update our knowledge base regularly, but always verify current rules with USCIS.

### What if I disagree with my recommendations?

The AI makes recommendations based on data you provide. Try:
1. Reviewing your answers for accuracy
2. Using the chat to ask specific questions
3. Consulting with an immigration attorney

### Can the system file applications for me?

No. This system provides guidance only. Actual filing requires working with an attorney or doing it yourself following official procedures.
